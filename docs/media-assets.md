## 2. Optional Motion / Social Prompts (Not Auto-Playing Heavy Video by Default)

To protect Core Web Vitals and constrained mobile connections, the storefront defaults to optimized still imagery. If the owner commissions or generates motion assets later:

- **8–12s Hero Loop Prompt:** Slow stabilized camera follow of an unbranded 6-passenger MPV on a Western Ghats road at dawn; left third kept low-detail; deliver with still poster frame and disable autoplay under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **9:16 Social Reel Prompt:** 12–15s Bengaluru pre-departure → scenic ghat drive → coffee estate walk → shared meal → landscape beat; mark people/places as illustrative unless shot on an actual permitted Sanchari trip.

## 3. Owner-Supplied Assets Needed Before Live Launch
